## Welcome to ZeroKeyUSB

This section walks you through the device screen by screen, with illustrations and the buttons you need to press at each step. No prior technical knowledge required — just the device and a USB-C cable.

## The five gold dots

Everything is controlled with five gold touch dots arranged in a cross beside the screen. Since firmware **1.1.0** you **swipe** across them (Gestures, the default); you can also press them one by one as buttons (**Menu → Settings → Control**). Both modes do the same five actions:

| Action | Gestures | Buttons | What it does |
|--------|----------|---------|--------------|
| **Up / Down** | Swipe down / up | Up / Down dot | Change what is in the band: previous / next field, option or value |
| **Left** | Swipe left to right | Left dot | Go back: previous account, previous step; from account 1, the menu |
| **Right** | Swipe right to left | Right dot | Go on: next account, next step; on the PIN, add the number |
| **Center** | Tap the centre | Centre dot | Act: type the field on the computer, open, apply |
| **Hold Center** | Hold the centre (~0.8 s) | Hold the centre dot | Edit the current field |

On your accounts, in the menu and in the setup, a swipe drags the screen like on a phone, so swiping up shows what is **below**. On the screens with a cursor (the on-device editor, the 2FA date and time, the search) the cursor simply follows your finger. See [Gestures](/getting-started/gestures) for all the moves, the PIN wheel and the tutorial.

The step-by-step guides below name the **actions** (Up, Down, Left, Right, Center). In Gestures mode, make the swipe from this table. Some of their illustrations still show the 1.0 screens; the steps are the same.

## Before you start

<Steps>
  <Step title="Plug in the USB-C cable">
    Plug ZeroKeyUSB into any USB-C port on your computer, tablet, or phone. The device draws about 20 mA — less than a wireless mouse — and needs no battery.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check the orientation">
    Hold the device the way that feels natural. During the initial setup you choose **Right-handed** or **Left-handed** and the screen turns round to match.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose your PIN">
    Before starting the initial tutorial it helps to have a 4–16 digit PIN in mind. If you forget it, **there is no recovery** — only wiping and starting over.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Conventions used in these guides

| Notation | Meaning |
|----------|---------|
| **Press X** | Short tap on button X (less than 800 ms) |
| **Hold X** | Long press on button X (more than 800 ms — you will see the cyan ring appear) |
| Cyan button in the illustration | The button to press to advance to the next step |
| Cyan button with large halo | Long press |
| `monospaced text` | Text that appears literally on the OLED screen |

<Note>
Want to try before you have the device in hand? Open the [online simulator](https://zerokeyusb.com/simulador.html): it reproduces the 1.1.0 screens. Drag the mouse (or your finger) across the dots to swipe, or use the arrow keys and Space.
</Note>
