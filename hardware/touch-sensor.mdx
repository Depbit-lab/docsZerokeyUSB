## No moving parts

ZeroKeyUSB has **no mechanical buttons**. Five gold contact points are wired to a **six-channel capacitive touch controller (TS06)**, which senses the finger through the device's resin surface. Nothing clicks, so nothing wears out mechanically.

---

## How it works

The TS06 measures small changes in capacitance on each contact point and reports, in one register, **every point that is being touched** (multi-output mode). The firmware reads that register every **10 ms** in Gestures mode (30 ms in Buttons mode) and works out what the finger did.

Because it reports all the points under the finger at once — a fingertip often covers two or three — the firmware can follow a finger **sliding across the points**, not only single presses. That is what makes [gestures](/getting-started/gestures) possible.

---

## Touch layout

The five gold points form a cross beside the screen:

| Point | Action (Buttons mode) | In Gestures mode |
|-------|-----------------------|------------------|
| **Up (↑)** | Up: previous field, option or value | Part of the vertical swipe |
| **Down (↓)** | Down: next field, option or value | Part of the vertical swipe |
| **Left (←)** | Back / previous | Part of the horizontal swipe |
| **Right (→)** | Go on / next | Part of the horizontal swipe |
| **Centre (•)** | Act on what is selected | Tap or hold it; every swipe goes through it |

Of the TS06's six channels, five are wired to the points; the sixth (CH1) is not connected. The controller also raises a noise flag, which the firmware ignores as a touch.

---

## Presses and gestures

| Input | How | Typical use |
|-------|-----|-------------|
| **Swipe** | Slide from one edge point, through the centre, to the opposite one | Move between accounts, options and steps |
| **Tap** | Centre, or the centre with a neighbour, or all five points | Type the field, open, apply |
| **Hold** | Centre for 0.8 s | Edit the field |
| **Hold to confirm** | Centre for 1.5 s on a confirmation page | Factory reset, bootloader, authorising an export |

In Buttons mode each point is a button instead: a press of at least 80 ms registers, and 800 ms makes it a long press. See [Touch Input Handling](/firmware/io-touch-handling) for the timings and rules.

---

## Visual feedback

- The screen slides in the direction of the swipe, so you see that the move was understood.
- A border fills around the screen while a hold is in progress.
- Touching an edge point alone shows a small picture of the swipe to make.

---

## Why touch instead of buttons?

| Feature | Physical buttons | ZeroKeyUSB touch points |
|---------|------------------|-------------------------|
| Mechanical wear | Yes | None |
| Splashes and dust | Gaps around the keys | Under the resin: splash and dust resistant (not rated for submersion) |
| Noise | Audible click | Silent |
| Small targets | Must hit the key | Gestures only need the direction |

---

## Sensitivity

The TS06 keeps no settings when it is powered off, so the firmware configures it on **every start** and reads the settings back to check them: the sensitivity of each channel, the multi-output mode, and releasing the channels the chip holds after a reset. The controller tracks each channel's baseline by itself.

The sensitivity is not identical on every point: the left point is the least sensitive and the right one the most. Gestures make this matter much less, because a swipe is judged by where the finger went, not by hitting one point exactly.
